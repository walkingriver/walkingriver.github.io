I've read and listened to a lot of books in my half-century on this planet. Most of them were not memorable in any way. There are a few, however, that stand out as having a profound impact on me and the way I life my life.

<!--more-->
![Books on a shelf](https://walkingriver.com/assets/img/a-book-3480216_1280.jpg)


Below is a list of each of those books that caused me to reconsider sincerely-held beliefs and opinions. It is not an exhaustive list. Though the books here are all non-fiction, there have been a few fiction books that also affected me. I may list them in another post some day.

Without further adieu,  here are my top six "most influential" books of my adult life. These books shaped who I am, informed many of my decisions, and continue to challenge how I think.

_Note: Links are Amazon affiliate links._

# [_Basic Economics_](https://amzn.to/35epmmz) by Thomas Sowell
<a href="https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00L4FSSTA?ie=UTF8&linkCode=li2&tag=twitter-std-20&linkId=2cddf51de6072859a433047bc9512a4d&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_il" target="_blank"><img border="0" src="//ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&ASIN=B00L4FSSTA&Format=_SL160_&ID=AsinImage&MarketPlace=US&ServiceVersion=20070822&WS=1&tag=twitter-std-20&language=en_US" ></a><img src="https://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=twitter-std-20&language=en_US&l=li2&o=1&a=B00L4FSSTA" width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" style="border:none !important; margin:0px !important;" />

Let's face it, Economics is a difficult and often dry subject. The book itself starts by defining Economics as "Economics is the study of the use of scarce resources which have alternative uses" Anything by Dr. Sowell is well-written, easy to comprehend, and worth your time. If everyone learned at least a little high-level economic theory, by reading this book, we could avoid many arguments. 


# [_How to Win Friends and Influence People_](https://amzn.to/2Igu8XM) by Dale Carnegie
<a href="https://www.amazon.com/How-Win-Friends-Influence-People-ebook/dp/B003WEAI4E?crid=1WQNP86Z25GMJ&dchild=1&keywords=how+to+win+friends+and+influence+people&qid=1604954156&s=digital-text&sprefix=how+to+win%2Cdigital-text%2C176&sr=1-3&linkCode=li2&tag=twitter-std-20&linkId=75ca05d0eb59fca68fb2f20197048996&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_il" target="_blank"><img border="0" src="//ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&ASIN=B003WEAI4E&Format=_SL160_&ID=AsinImage&MarketPlace=US&ServiceVersion=20070822&WS=1&tag=twitter-std-20&language=en_US" ></a><img src="https://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=twitter-std-20&language=en_US&l=li2&o=1&a=B003WEAI4E" width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" style="border:none !important; margin:0px !important;" />
This one made me realize how much of a jerk I can sometimes be to people without knowing it. Read it to be a better all-around person.

# [_Loserthink_](https://amzn.to/38qBBOV) by [Scott Adams](https://twitter.com/ScottAdamsSays)
<a href="https://www.amazon.com/Loserthink-Untrained-Brains-Ruining-America-ebook/dp/B07P8J8YB6?crid=4GOUN1W3OVG8&dchild=1&keywords=loserthink+scott+adams&qid=1604954254&s=digital-text&sprefix=lower+think%2Cdigital-text%2C157&sr=1-1&linkCode=li2&tag=twitter-std-20&linkId=643beca9d5f5398852e126d5951e3c04&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_il" target="_blank"><img border="0" src="//ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&ASIN=B07P8J8YB6&Format=_SL160_&ID=AsinImage&MarketPlace=US&ServiceVersion=20070822&WS=1&tag=twitter-std-20&language=en_US" ></a><img src="https://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=twitter-std-20&language=en_US&l=li2&o=1&a=B07P8J8YB6" width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" style="border:none !important; margin:0px !important;" />
This one is a late-entry to my list, as I only discovered it this year. 


The book caused me to rethink how I think. Even though the author and I don't always agree, he has caused me to pause and ask myself why.

# [_The Richest Man in Babylon_](https://amzn.to/36nrKH9) by George S. Clason 
<a href="https://www.amazon.com/Richest-Man-Babylon-Magic-Story/dp/1939438632?_encoding=UTF8&qid=1604954393&sr=1-5&linkCode=li2&tag=twitter-std-20&linkId=ea5e0b397fd274ab4de6b01f4595d977&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_il" target="_blank"><img border="0" src="//ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&ASIN=1939438632&Format=_SL160_&ID=AsinImage&MarketPlace=US&ServiceVersion=20070822&WS=1&tag=twitter-std-20&language=en_US" ></a><img src="https://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=twitter-std-20&language=en_US&l=li2&o=1&a=1939438632" width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" style="border:none !important; margin:0px !important;" />
It is a book of financial fables, each designed to change how you think about money and its role in life. Very easy to read, and contains lessons for all of us.

# [_Rich Dad, Poor Dad_](https://amzn.to/32pMqNh) by Robert Kiyosaki 
<a href="https://www.amazon.com/Rich-Dad-Poor-Teach-Middle-ebook/dp/B07C7M8SX9?crid=W6E3LII8Q2O9&dchild=1&keywords=rich+dad+poor+dad&qid=1604954503&s=digital-text&sprefix=rich+dad%2Cdigital-text%2C168&sr=1-3&linkCode=li2&tag=twitter-std-20&linkId=3796f0cde0ab060e0c556eea3b6e45d8&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_il" target="_blank"><img border="0" src="//ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&ASIN=B07C7M8SX9&Format=_SL160_&ID=AsinImage&MarketPlace=US&ServiceVersion=20070822&WS=1&tag=twitter-std-20&language=en_US" ></a><img src="https://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=twitter-std-20&language=en_US&l=li2&o=1&a=B07C7M8SX9" width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" style="border:none !important; margin:0px !important;" />

The entire series is excellent, though the first two are the best. I took the wrong lesson from this one, as I admit in my blog post about real estate investing failures. I treasure the book and what it taught me.

# [_The Federalist Papers_](https://amzn.to/2IoaBF7) by Alexander Hamilton (et al)
<a href="https://www.amazon.com/Federalist-Papers-AmazonClassics-Alexander-Hamilton-ebook/dp/B075173F15?crid=2BCSU2YABPYOL&dchild=1&keywords=federalist+papers&qid=1604955065&s=digital-text&sprefix=federali%2Cdigital-text%2C158&sr=1-3&linkCode=li2&tag=twitter-std-20&linkId=95748257f4913a3feb4c1ee61f2f4efd&language=en_US&ref_=as_li_ss_il" target="_blank"><img border="0" src="//ws-na.amazon-adsystem.com/widgets/q?_encoding=UTF8&ASIN=B075173F15&Format=_SL160_&ID=AsinImage&MarketPlace=US&ServiceVersion=20070822&WS=1&tag=twitter-std-20&language=en_US" ></a><img src="https://ir-na.amazon-adsystem.com/e/ir?t=twitter-std-20&language=en_US&l=li2&o=1&a=B075173F15" width="1" height="1" border="0" alt="" style="border:none !important; margin:0px !important;" />

Read this book If you truly want to understand why the US was founded as it is. The language can be a bit hard to understand at times, but it's worth the effort.
